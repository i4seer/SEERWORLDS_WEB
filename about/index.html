About the author

B. Carter

Stories about ordinary people facing strange rules, impossible choices, and worlds that never work quite the way they expect.

Writer · engineer · builder

Where story meets curiosity.

B. Carter is the author behind Seer Worlds, Seer Frights, and Seer Warns—three series that explore wonder, fear, strange rules, and the choices people make when reality stops behaving.

A Native American computer engineer and veteran with a PhD in Computer Engineering and Computer Science, he brings a builder’s curiosity to every story: How does this world work? What happens when one rule changes? What would a real person do next?

The tone shifts from the adventurous mystery of Seer Worlds, to the creepy fun of Seer Frights, to the quieter moral horror of Seer Warns. The question underneath them is the same: what do ordinary people do when they encounter something impossible?
