Series update

Books 1–9 are complete. New releases every week.

The first nine Seer Worlds books are complete, from Distance through Promises. The remaining books in this first arc will be published one each week, bringing a new world, a new rule, and another step in the Dot’s journey. Explore the books →

September 2026
New releases

Time and Maps are now available.

Time (Book 5) visits a valley that lives at two different speeds. Maps (Book 6) follows a courier whose map shows a road nobody can find. Both are out now, joining Books 1–4 on the shelf.

September 2026
Cover update

The current shelf reaches Book 9.

The redesigned Seer Worlds cover line now runs from Distance through Promises, using one consistent visual system: white space, one defining object, and the Dot.

Books 8 & 9

Shadows and Promises join the shelf.

Shadows follows a world where shadows have lives of their own. Promises closes the first nine-book arc with a world where a promise becomes something you carry.

Series plan

Arc One spans nine worlds.

From Distance through Promises, each world stands on its own while the Dot keeps following the larger question beneath them.
