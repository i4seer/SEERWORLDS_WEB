About B. Carter

B. Carter writes stories about ordinary people facing strange rules, impossible choices, and worlds that never work quite the way they expect.

He is the author of Seer Worlds, a middle-grade fantasy series following a small floating traveler called the Dot; Seer Frights, middle-grade horror built around creepy places and dangerous things; and Seer Warns, adult literary horror about warnings, choices, and what they cost.

B. Carter is a Native American computer engineer, veteran, and the author of the Seer series. He holds a PhD in Computer Engineering and Computer Science and has a habit of building things that probably shouldn’t exist.

When he isn’t writing, he’s coding, hiking, reading horror, comics, manga, and adventure stories—or dreaming up new ways to build things that probably shouldn’t work.
