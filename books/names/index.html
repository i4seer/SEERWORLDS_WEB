ORRUN • THE CITY UNDER STONE

Every child chooses a name.

Every name shapes a body.

Ganno chose the wrong one.

The Dot reaches Underhand, a city carved beneath a mountain, where every child chooses what they will become. Speak a name and the body changes to match it—Carrier, Wallwalker, Deepdigger. The choice lasts for life.

Pell cannot wait to choose. Ganno chose decades ago and became the finest digger in Underhand. Everyone admires the body he has. No one sees the life he wanted glowing just beyond his reach.

The Dot comes looking for a broken rule it can fix. Instead it must learn what help cannot repair, what listening can still give, and whether any name can contain a whole person.

A name can shape a body. It cannot contain a life.

The question

Is what you are something you discover, or something you choose?

A world with its own rule

Each Seer Worlds book is designed to stand on its own. The local problem belongs to the world first; the larger series mystery remains underneath the story instead of replacing it.

For curious readers

Short chapters, clear language, strange ideas, quiet humor, and enough mystery to keep the next world calling.
