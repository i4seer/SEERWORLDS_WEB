BELL • THE WHITE CLIFF

Every road has an owner.

Every Vey can feel the border.

The Dot crosses it anyway.

The Dot is small, round, and floating. It does not know what it is or why it travels between worlds. On Bell, an entire civilization clings to shelves carved into a white cliff—and every stretch of road belongs to someone.

When a neighbor claims the only route Mara's family uses to reach market, the claim is legal, the border invisible, and losing four hundred twelve steps could cost them their home.

Then the Dot finds an abandoned ledge with no owner at all. The road should be impossible. Crossing it may save Mara's family—or prove that the rule holding Bell together was never a rule of the world.

A road can be owned. A question cannot.

The question

What if a rule everyone treats as physics is really an agreement?

A world with its own rule

Each Seer Worlds book is designed to stand on its own. The local problem belongs to the world first; the larger series mystery remains underneath the story instead of replacing it.

For curious readers

Short chapters, clear language, strange ideas, quiet humor, and enough mystery to keep the next world calling.
