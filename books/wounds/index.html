WOUNDS

A wound closes before anyone can clean it. Keeping the door shut has a cost too.

Planned

Here living edges join the moment they touch: the orchards have grown together, and so do wounds, over whatever dirt is still inside them. Thirty-seven people died of that in this district last year. Behind the healing house is a door shut three hundred years ago after twenty-two deaths, and everyone agreed that was right, and nobody ever counted what leaving it shut would cost.

The question

Is staying cautious ever actually the safe choice, or just the choice that feels safest to make?

A world with its own rule

Each Seer Worlds book is designed to stand on its own. The local problem belongs to the world first; the larger series mystery remains underneath the story instead of replacing it.

For curious readers

Short chapters, clear language, strange ideas, quiet humor, and enough mystery to keep the next world calling.
