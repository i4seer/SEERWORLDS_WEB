SETH • THE WORK ROW

Every shadow tells the truth.

Everyone knows not to look.

The Dot says what it sees.

On Seth, shadows reveal the private thoughts their owners never speak. Everyone can read them. Children learn early to look away.

The Dot doesn't know that rule. At Hannit's repair stall, it watches thirteen-year-old Tavi work, joke, and carry a business that depends on them. Then Tavi's shadow lifts a travel bundle and starts walking east whenever the next season is mentioned.

When the Dot names what everyone has agreed not to see, the stall falls silent and Tavi stops coming. Repairing the harm will take more than an apology. It will mean learning that seeing the truth does not make it yours to tell—and that sometimes a whole town must build room for a person to choose.

Some truths need shade before they can be spoken.

The question

What do you owe somebody whose secret you can already see?

A world with its own rule

Each Seer Worlds book is designed to stand on its own. The local problem belongs to the world first; the larger series mystery remains underneath the story instead of replacing it.

For curious readers

Short chapters, clear language, strange ideas, quiet humor, and enough mystery to keep the next world calling.
