WEAR

A tool learns one person's hands and refuses everyone else's.

Planned

The Dot finds a wheel that will not turn for the person turning it, because on this world work settles into things: a tool used the same way for twenty years gets better at that and worse at anything else. People do not settle, and that part matters. Kavi has run this water gate for seventeen years and is very good at it, and she is leaving, and the town has nine weeks to decide what it is owed.

The question

Does being excellent at something for a long time create a duty to keep doing it?

A world with its own rule

Each Seer Worlds book is designed to stand on its own. The local problem belongs to the world first; the larger series mystery remains underneath the story instead of replacing it.

For curious readers

Short chapters, clear language, strange ideas, quiet humor, and enough mystery to keep the next world calling.
