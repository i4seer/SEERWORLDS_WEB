UNANSWERED

Somebody has been waiting three hundred years to catch whatever arrives.

Planned

At the Fetching Ground, things arrive: objects, plants, animals, and once or twice in a lifetime something confused and alive. They have turned up on a swept stone floor for three hundred years without warning and without explanation, so an entire town has grown up around the work of receiving whatever comes. Nobody here asks why. Nothing has ever answered — until now, something is waiting to catch the Dot.

The question

Can a place be home before you know why it was built to receive you?

A world with its own rule

Each Seer Worlds book is designed to stand on its own. The local problem belongs to the world first; the larger series mystery remains underneath the story instead of replacing it.

For curious readers

Short chapters, clear language, strange ideas, quiet humor, and enough mystery to keep the next world calling.
