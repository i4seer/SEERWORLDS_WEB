MAPS

A map shows the routes available to its reader.

The Reach is an open lattice of stone ribs through clear sky. A courier’s map shows a road nobody can find, and proving the map is right turns out to be very different from making anything better.

The question

What can proof give you—and what can it never give back?

Why this world matters

Each Seer Worlds book is complete on its own while also adding one piece to the Dot’s larger journey. The rule shapes ordinary life first; the mystery stays underneath the local story.
