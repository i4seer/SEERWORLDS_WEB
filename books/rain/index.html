RAIN

Cutting the mountain moves rain from somewhere else to here.

Planned

The Dot finds six people cutting a mountain open to make it rain harder, because fresh stone pulls rain: not new water, the same water, bent away from somewhere else. Sixteen years ago a fire stripped this ridge and the cutting brought the rain back, and it worked, and every closing condition has been met. So today they make the last cut. Downhill, seventy-three households now live on rain that was only ever borrowed.

The question

If a solution works perfectly, does anyone still have to ask who is paying for it?

A world with its own rule

Each Seer Worlds book is designed to stand on its own. The local problem belongs to the world first; the larger series mystery remains underneath the story instead of replacing it.

For curious readers

Short chapters, clear language, strange ideas, quiet humor, and enough mystery to keep the next world calling.
