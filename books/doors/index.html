SULL • THE SHALLOW BANK

Every door remembers who belongs.

Rell's is holding someone else.

The Dot asks why.

The Dot arrives beneath a warm, shallow sea where young Kelt race between shell-homes carrying messages. When they grow up, they settle forever—and every home remembers exactly whom its owner welcomed.

At the cold edge lives Rell. No one visits her. Her door has narrowed almost shut, yet someone has been trapped behind it for twenty years.

The Dot wants to help. Instead it asks questions in the one place where nothing can stay private, turning Rell's secret into the bank's accusation. To put things right, the Dot must learn why doors close, how trust opens, and when an answer belongs to someone else.

Some doors open only when someone comes back.

The question

What is the difference between letting somebody in and truly welcoming them?

A world with its own rule

Each Seer Worlds book is designed to stand on its own. The local problem belongs to the world first; the larger series mystery remains underneath the story instead of replacing it.

For curious readers

Short chapters, clear language, strange ideas, quiet humor, and enough mystery to keep the next world calling.
