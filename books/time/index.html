VELL • THE TWO FORDS

The same river runs at two speeds.

The same promise spans eleven years.

The wait was never the same.

The Dot reaches a green valley divided by an invisible line. On one bank, days move five times faster than on the other. Everyone knows the arithmetic. Living it is harder.

Sella runs the ferry on the quick side. Bern tends an ancient orchard on the long. They promised as children to meet at every turning of the river—and Bern has never missed one. Yet for each season he remembers, Sella has waited nearly a year.

The Dot believes the right schedule can close the distance. But friendship is not a parcel to deliver, and equal dates cannot make two lives feel the same.

Time can be counted. Waiting has to be lived.

The question

What happens when two incompatible accounts are both true?

A world with its own rule

Each Seer Worlds book is designed to stand on its own. The local problem belongs to the world first; the larger series mystery remains underneath the story instead of replacing it.

For curious readers

Short chapters, clear language, strange ideas, quiet humor, and enough mystery to keep the next world calling.
