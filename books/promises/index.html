THE LANDINGS • THE WIDE RIVER

He gave her a future she could see.

She grew into a different life.

The promise never changed.

On the Landings, every promise becomes a weight the maker must carry until it is kept—or broken.

Years ago, Corrin promised eleven-year-old Nilla the patched riverboat that gave her a future she could see. Now seventeen, Nilla has found the work she loves repairing rope. She has refused the boat six times. The promise remains on Corrin's back, too large for either of them to ignore.

The Dot is certain there must be a kinder fourth choice. But some promises cannot be transferred, revised, or taken back. As an old undertaking reveals why the roads between worlds were closed, the Dot must learn that breaking a good promise may be the only way to honor the person it once protected.

A promise can be good—and still need to end.

The question

What do you do when every available choice is wrong?

A world with its own rule

Each Seer Worlds book is designed to stand on its own. The local problem belongs to the world first; the larger series mystery remains underneath the story instead of replacing it.

For curious readers

Short chapters, clear language, strange ideas, quiet humor, and enough mystery to keep the next world calling.
