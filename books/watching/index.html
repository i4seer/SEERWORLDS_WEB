WATCHING

You cannot measure a bridge you are watching.

Planned

Somebody shouts at the Dot not to look at the bridge, because here being watched holds a thing together, and you cannot measure a bridge you are watching. Thirty-one people stand on the platform with their backs turned and their eyes shut. One thing is facing the span — it floats, it is not a person, and it is not the Dot. It is the second one of its kind the Dot has ever met, and it has a job it chose.

The question

If someone freely chooses the same work you were afraid was forced on you, does that change what the work means?

A world with its own rule

Each Seer Worlds book is designed to stand on its own. The local problem belongs to the world first; the larger series mystery remains underneath the story instead of replacing it.

For curious readers

Short chapters, clear language, strange ideas, quiet humor, and enough mystery to keep the next world calling.
