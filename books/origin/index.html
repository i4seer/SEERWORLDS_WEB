Planned

Above a market, sunlight makes stone lighter, and a whole city balances its roofs and lifts against the hour of the day. One new awning shaded one old bay for twelve days. Nobody did anything wrong. And when they open the market floor to see what the leaning wall is standing on, they find five sealed rooms and a list of six names.

The question

Does knowing where you began tell you what you should do now?

A world with its own rule

Each Seer Worlds book is designed to stand on its own. The local problem belongs to the world first; the larger series mystery remains underneath the story instead of replacing it.
