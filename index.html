Meet the traveler

A small traveler.
A very large question.

The Dot is smooth, reflective, literal, patient, and curious. It can cross from world to world. It can measure what other people take for granted. It does not know why it can do any of those things.

The larger story grows quietly beneath each standalone adventure, but the world in front of the Dot always comes first.
