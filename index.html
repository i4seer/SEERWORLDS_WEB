Meet the traveler

The Dot is small.
The questions are not.

The Dot can float. It can measure distance very precisely. It can cross from one world to another. It does not know why.

It has no map, no home address, and no special talent for leaving well enough alone. When a world says, “That’s just how things work,” the Dot tends to ask one more question.

About the author

B. Carter

B. Carter writes stories about ordinary people facing strange rules, impossible choices, and worlds that never work quite the way they expect.

He is the author of Seer Worlds, the middle-grade fantasy journey of the Dot; Seer Frights, middle-grade horror full of creepy places and dangerous things; and Seer Warns, adult literary horror about choices, warnings, and what they cost.

B. Carter is a Native American computer engineer and veteran. He holds a PhD in Computer Engineering and Computer Science and has a habit of building things that probably shouldn’t exist.

When he isn’t writing, he’s coding, hiking, reading horror, comics, manga, and adventure stories—or dreaming up new ways to build things that probably shouldn’t work.
