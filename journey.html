The questions get bigger.

The Dot begins by learning how worlds work. Eventually the questions turn toward home, belonging, and responsibility.

What are the worlds?

2

Where does the Dot belong?

Origin, home, and belonging are not the same thing. The next part of the journey asks the Dot to learn the difference.

3

Book 15+

What does the Dot owe?

Being able to cross worlds eventually means someone will ask the Dot to carry something between them.
